title: The Land History Forgot

---

The land of Ironroot endured despite the catastrophe, and began to flourish in ways it hadn't since mankind climbed the ladders from primitive civilizations. Remnants of the
old world lingered like scars across the planet, but the animals had returned, and in abundance. In the shadows of these ancient ruins, in sweeping forests, vast plains and
deep oceans, they thrived once more. 

Ironroot had won again, and it always would.
