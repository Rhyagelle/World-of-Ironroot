Before there was a hall, there was a kiln. The first survivors fired clay in a pit near the marsh because a pot that held water mattered more than any wall. The people who kept that fire burning became the Wardens.

## What they do

The Wardens are not rulers. They keep the storm records, mediate disputes and decide when a stretch of road is safe enough to reopen. Any settler can ask to join them, and the only test is whether they will keep the fire lit when it is inconvenient.

## The Oath

New Wardens recite a short oath at the kiln. It has never been written down, which is why no two versions match, and why the Wardens argue about it every winter.

> A settler who asks for shelter is given a place by the fire. A settler who is given a place by the fire is trusted with the kiln.
