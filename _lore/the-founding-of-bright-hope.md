---
title: The founding of Bright Hope
order: 4
summary: Where the Coalition first arrived.
---

The Coalition's arrival on Ironroot was uneasy, and yet filled with hope at the revitalized state of the world, and the distinct lack of the enemy. The first establishment
of the Allied Races is built along the coastline, and is named “Bright Hope”. 

Bright Hope is the hub of the new world. From here, you must strive forward and seek the mysteries of the enemy, and conquer the nature of Ironroot once more.
