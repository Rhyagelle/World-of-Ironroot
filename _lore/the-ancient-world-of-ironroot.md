---
title: The Ancient world of Ironroot
order: 1
summary: How the world collapsed beneath the Convergence.
---

The World of Ironroot is ancient in its years. It has seen the rise and fall of mankind many times, and it is always the last standing. The planet is a harsh environment to
survive in, but once you have conquered it, it rewards you in abundance. With all great things, come greater risks, however.

The world faced a new threat, and it was, for once, a threat that mankind could not keep at bay. The people called this terror many names, but it was commonly known as the
“Convergence”. The Convergence came as violent storms that bridged the dimensional gap between Ironroot and that of a twisted, dark dimension. 

The enemy comes and goes with these storms, fading in and out of reality as if smoke in the wind. For sixty years, the world fought back. However, in the year 1123, came a
storm so devastating that none could escape its fury. It tore across the planet, reshaping parts of the world and releasing beasts of untold horror.

On the seventy third year after the first storm, mankind built a machine they called “The Gate”. It harvested the energy of the storms and created dimensional rifts of their
own. The technology was unstable, and each time they powered it on, the rift showed a different world. 

Although they did not know if their desperate act would bring them to salvation or into darkness itself, the remaining peoples of Ironroot fled through The Gate and into a
new world.
