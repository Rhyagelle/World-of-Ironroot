Hollowmere sits in a bowl of land that every earlier settlement avoided. It floods in spring. It holds the cold. Nothing grows well there until the second year.

## Why here

The survivors who chose it had counted the options and found that every dry, sunny valley had already been claimed by something worse. A flooded valley could be drained, and the marsh clay was perfect for the kiln. They stayed because the other choices had run out.

## The first winter

They built the hall before the walls, and the walls before the roofs. It was the wrong order and everyone knew it. They did it anyway, because the hall was where people could sit together and the rest could wait.

Three of the first twelve did not see spring. Their names are carved over the hall door, which is why the door is never locked.

## What came next

The market, the road to the ridge, the long argument about who should hold the tin. All of it is still being written, and your character is part of that story.
