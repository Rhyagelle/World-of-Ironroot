You are among a small portion of men and women who have agreed to leave the safety and warmth of the new world of Tinaris to walk through The Gate and back into Ironroot, on
the sole hope of reclaiming your ancestorial home world, and to learn at last what the Enemy truly is, and how to end their threat once and for all. 

The travel through the Gate can only allow organic material through. You and your fellows—a coalition of diverse specialists—are sent through with nothing more than the
clothes on your back, and wishes of your people.

Will you succumb to the everlasting presence of the enemy, or will you conquer all before you?
