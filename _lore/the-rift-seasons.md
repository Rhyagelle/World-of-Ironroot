The first storm came in as a bruise on the horizon. By nightfall the air had a taste, like a coin held on the tongue, and the ground had begun to behave in ways ground should not.

## How a storm arrives

Survivors learned the signs quickly. Birdsong stops. The light flattens. Shadows point the wrong way for a few breaths before the sky changes colour. After that there is only time to reach shelter.

> Storms are part of the setting, and they are dangerous. Find cover as soon as the warning appears, and don't wait to see how bad this one is.
{: .callout .warn}

## What they leave behind

Each storm leaves the reaches slightly changed. Roads fade. Familiar landmarks drift out of place. Strange things surface from the ground afterwards: odd stones, broken machinery, now and then something that looks like it was made last week.

The Wardens keep a record of every storm. It is the only reliable calendar left.

### The long quiet between

Between storms the reaches feel almost ordinary, which is when most people make their mistakes. The settlers of Hollowmere learned to treat calm weather as a gift to spend on building, not as a sign of safety.
