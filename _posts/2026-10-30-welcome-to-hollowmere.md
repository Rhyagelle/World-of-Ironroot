The server site is live. Here is what you'll find.

- **Lore** covers the setting, from the last clear sky to the founding of the settlement.
- **Mods** lists everything running here and marks which ones you need to join.
- **Map** shows where everyone has built, with coordinates you can copy.

If you're new, start with the **Join** page. If you've played before, check the mod list, since a few things have changed.

Welcome in, and watch the sky.
