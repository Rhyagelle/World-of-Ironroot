layout: page
title: Rules
lede: Short and simple. They apply to everyone, including admins.
---

1. **Be decent to other players.** No harassment, slurs or targeted abuse in chat, in Discord or in the world.
2. **Don't grief.** Never destroy, steal from or build over another player's claimed land. If you're unsure, ask first.
3. **Claim what you build.** Use the claim tools to protect your base. Unclaimed structures within a few blocks of a claim may be built around.
4. **Share the shared places.** Quarries, roads and the market are for everyone. Take what you need and tidy up after you.
5. **Keep your builds in step with the world.** Large structures should suit the setting. Check with an admin before building something that dominates the skyline.
6. **No cheating.** Duplication exploits, x-ray clients and unapproved mods are a ban. Report bugs instead of using them.
7. **Respect the storms.** Temporal storms are part of the setting. Don't use commands or exploits to avoid them for yourself or others.
8. **Listen to the admins.** Decisions are explained when asked. Disputes go to Discord, not into chat.
{: .rules}

> Breaking a rule once by accident is a conversation. Breaking it on purpose is a ban.
{: .callout}
