layout: page
title: Rules
lede: Short and simple. They apply to everyone, including admins.
---

1. **Be decent to other players.** No harassment, slurs or targeted abuse in chat, in Discord or in the world.
2. **Don't grief.** Never destroy, steal from or build over another player's claimed land. If you're unsure, ask first.
3. **Claim what you build.** Use the claim tools to protect your base! In cases where this system fails, the admin/support team will be here to help you. 
4. **Share the shared places.** Story locations and merchants are for everyone. You are NOT allowed to claim these areas. You may claim the area around them, but you MUST provide access to the story locations, dungeons and merchants, as the first two WILL be reset to give people the chance to enjoy the content.
5. **Keep your builds in step with the world.** The rules on this are very lax, we only ask that you do not build "ugly" on purpose, so no dirt pillars, or mud-huts you do not intend to update. Waterways may have bridges built or other kinds of modication, but you must ensure that the water is clear enough to allow boats to sail through.
6. **No cheating.** Duplication exploits and x-ray clients are a ban. Unapproved mods will just kick you. Report bugs instead of using them.
7. **Respect the storms.** Temporal storms are part of the setting. Don't use commands or exploits to avoid them for yourself or others.
8. **Listen to the admins.** Decisions are explained when asked. Disputes go to Discord, not into chat.

   These rules are subject to change and are not finalized. 
{: .rules}

> Breaking a rule once by accident is a conversation. Breaking it on purpose is a ban. Just please be decent.
{: .callout}
